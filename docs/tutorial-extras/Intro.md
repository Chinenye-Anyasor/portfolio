# Introduction

My name is Chinenye Precious Anyasor, and I have a bachelor's in human physiology. By now, I thought I would be working in a lab somewhere, but alas, life had its plans.

Before I developed an interest in tech documentation, I spent two years as a human resources professional in very fast-paced environments, at a YC-backed fintech and a large BPO. There, I was told that my superpowers are process ownership and the ability to pick up new tech with ease. Those skills tie neatly into tech documentation.

Personally, I have been off social media for the past six months, learning the trade secrets of tech documentation. What I did not expect was the tunnel vision and focus that came with it. Now I cannot decide whether or not to go back.

I love reading, cliché, right? But I do. Mostly fiction nowadays; I used to read history and autobiography.

Since I started learning tech documentation, I have taken several technical communication courses and put what I learned into practice. I wrote a set of getting started guides for a stock-trading app, covering how to create an account, fund it, buy stocks, and sell stocks. I added an FAQ page, a style guide, and a case study explaining how I approached the work. I also built this portfolio website myself with Docusaurus, and I write technical explainers and personal essays on my blog.

I have wanted to travel for the longest time to Spain, Italy, France, and Thailand — you know, places with history.

Anyway, I am fun, super collaborative, and a terrific researcher. I can work remotely, and I have a strong itch to get undeniably good at writing documentation.
