# Meritrade FAQs

This FAQ covers all the errors I encountered while testing out the user workflows mapped in the different guides.
* ### Can I use an email address previously used to open a Meritrade account to open a new account?
  No, you cannot. The system will flag it. Kindly create a new email address to complete your account opening.

* ### I did not get a code in my email for the email verification step in the registration process. What do I do?
  Contact the support team at this number: 08134059788 (Contacting the support team on WhatsApp is ideal)

* ### I did not receive a code in my SMS inbox for the phone verification step in the registration process. What do I do?
  Contact the support team at this number: 08134059788 (Contacting the support team on WhatsApp is ideal)

* ### I did not receive my phone verification code. I waited, then restarted the account opening process, but the app says my email is already linked to an existing account. What do I do?
  Your email is already linked to an existing account, so instead of clicking on `Create account`, click on `Login` to complete your account opening process.

* ### I did not receive the payment receipts from both Meritrade and Paystack after funding my wallet. What do I do?
  Kindly wait 24 hours and then contact the support team if you still do not receive the receipts in your email.

* ### The amount I funded my account with did not reflect on my available balance. What do I do?
  Kindly wait 24 hours and then contact the support team if the problem persists.

* ### The stock I want to buy is not available on Meritrade. What do I do?
  Confirm that the company whose stock you want to buy is listed on the Nigerian Stock Exchange, note the ticker symbol, use it to search on Meritrade, and if it is still not available, contact the support team.

* ### I want to place a buy order, but I keep getting an insufficient funds message. What do I do?
  Kindly fund your account and try again.

* ### Is the Meritrade app available offline?
  No, it is not. To access the app, you need internet connectivity.
